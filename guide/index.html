CopyHex

How to use

CopyHex is a free workspace for designers, developers and everyone who creates with color.

01

Colors

Tip: search a color family in your language or paste HEX, RGB or HSL. English color names work too.

02

Discover palettes

Explore considered color combinations. Make a copy, adjust every shade, and make it your own.

03

Palette generator

Choose a starting color, harmony and mood. Keep any shade by locking it. Regenerate the rest, change one column, compare alternatives and save a personal copy. Harmony rules guide exploration; review contrast for your specific use.

04

Color studio

Fine-tune every channel. Copy every format. Save your next signature color.

05

Contrast checker

WCAG AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text; AAA requires 7:1. For large text, AA requires 3:1. Review actual pairs and never communicate meaning through color alone.

06

Create a palette from an image

Choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP. Colors are extracted on this device; your image is never uploaded.

07

Export palette

Use CSS for implementation, JSON for data, SVG for editable graphics and PNG for design review. Export the full collection backup separately from My palettes.

08

Export backup

Palettes, favorites and settings stay in this browser without device sync. Export a backup before clearing browser data. Images are processed on your device.
